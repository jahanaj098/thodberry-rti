Submit Your RTI Reply

Help build India's public RTI knowledge base. Share your RTI reply with the community.

📋 Submission Guidelines

Before submitting, please ensure:

  • Your RTI reply was legally obtained under the RTI Act, 2005
  • The document is in PDF or image format (JPG, PNG)
  • File size is less than 10MB
  • You have the right to share this information publicly
  • No personal information (phone, address) of individuals is visible

Your submission will be reviewed by our team before publication. We may mask any personal information found in the document.

Why Contribute Your RTI Reply?

🌟 Help Others

Your RTI could answer someone else's question. Prevent duplicate requests and save time for everyone.

📊 Build Trends

Your contribution helps identify patterns across departments and strengthens public accountability.

💪 Strengthen Transparency

Every RTI shared makes government information more accessible and permanent.
